# fa5_creativity_app

POKÉMON CHAMPION DEX - AN APP FOR ASPIRING AND EXPERIENCED TRAINERS TO HONE AND TEST THEIR KNOWLEDGE AND BECOME A POKÉMON CHAMPION

API USED: https://pokeapi.co/

Use That Pokémon! (Pokémon Team Builder) allows trainers to look at different mixes of pokémon and their type coverage, Choose That Pokémon! (Pokémon Matchup Analyzer) shows the trainer how well a certain pokémon does as an attacker or defender against any pokémon, while also giving a general overview of its base stats, pokédex entry, possible abilities, and movepool, and Who's That Pokémon? (Pokémon Quiz) presents the trainer the classic 'Who's that Pokémon?' quiz from the anime. Design is specifically developed for Samsung Galaxy A55 (360 pixels x 800 pixels).
