# fa5_creativity_app

POKEMON CHAMPION DEX - AN APP FOR ASPIRING AND EXPERIENCED TRAINERS TO HONE AND TEST THEIR KNOWLEDGE

API USED: https://pokeapi.co/

Use That Pokemon! (Pokemon Team Builder) allows trainers to look at different mixes of pokemon and their type coverage, Choose That Pokemon! (Pokemon Matchup Analyzer) shows the trainer how well a certain pokemon does as an attacker or defender against any pokemon, while also giving a general overview of its base stats, pokedex entry, possible abilities, and movepool, and Who's That Pokemon? (Pokemon Quiz) presents the trainer the classic 'Who's that Pokemon?' quiz from the anime. Design is specifically developed for Samsung Galaxy A55 (360 pixels x 800 pixels).
